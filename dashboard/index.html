Token Launch Radar

Pre-token protocols ranked by what their launch would be worth — and how to farm each one.

How the value is derived — model

Every protocol that already trades prices its category: we take the median market-cap / TVL multiple of the token-bearing peers in the snapshot, then price each pre-token protocol's TVL at its own category's multiple. Three snapshot-native adjustments follow: 7-day growth (clamped ±30%, weight 0.8), tokenless age (up to +25% over three years) and chain breadth (+1% per extra chain, up to eight). Wrapped, staked and bridged products of traded tokens are excluded, never ranked. No name is special-cased: impliedValue() in the model scores every row with the same arithmetic the board carries, and the detail view shows each factor for the row you open.
